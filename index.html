    About the data

    Sources

    R areas: Finnish eAIP, ENR 5.1 (AIRAC 004-2024, 8 August 2024). D areas: Traficom regulation OPS M1-28, annex 1 (2023). UAS zones: Traficom UAS zone data, version 2026-10-5. Aerial photography restriction areas: the public areas file from the Finnish Defence Forces (listed there as updated 4 March 2026).

    Not included

    Prohibited areas (EFP) and the flight plan buffer zones (EFR…Z) are left out. EFR100 and EFD300 follow national borders, and the source gives no coordinates for those border legs, so only their listed points are shown, drawn as open lines.

    Coordinates

    All coordinates are WGS84, which matches ETRS89 to about a metre in Finland. MGRS and UTM values are truncated, the other formats are rounded. The R and D sources give whole seconds (about 30 m), so extra digits only reflect that. Areas that cross UTM zones show different zone designators.

    Check before you fly

    This is an unofficial compilation and may be out of date. The aerial photography layer shows only the public areas. Always confirm against the current AIP, NOTAMs and publications from Traficom and the Defence Forces.
