    About the data

    Sources

    R areas come from the Finnish eAIP, ENR 5.1 (AIRAC 004-2024, 8 August 2024). D areas come from Traficom regulation OPS M1-28, annex 1 (2023).

    Not included

    Prohibited areas (EFP) and the flight plan buffer zones (EFR…Z) are left out. EFR100 and EFD300 follow national borders, and the source gives no coordinates for those border legs, so only their listed inner boundary points are shown and drawn as lines.

    Precision

    The source coordinates are whole seconds, about 30 m. MGRS values are truncated, not rounded, and areas that cross UTM zones show different zone designators.

    Check before you fly

    This is an unofficial compilation and may be out of date. Always confirm against the current AIP, NOTAMs and Traficom publications.
